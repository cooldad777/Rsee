## Offer
**One sentence.** The commercial arm of SEE, R: a person in the room for a question, or a dated layer that leaves.

**What it is.** An umbrella over two named doors. **SEER Consulting** is human counsel — hour or question — with analytics inside the work. **SEER Install** is a client-owned markdown layer: install, train, dated exit. Future offers earn a name before they earn a URL.

**What it is not.** Not the hub. Not a résumé. Not Seer. Not a chatbot retainer. Not GROUNDED as SaaS. Not Adventure Within. Not a third door smuggled in as “AI coaching.”

**Who it is for.** A midsized operator in the Southeast who is data-rich and insight-poor, and who will stay in the room. Operations-heavy firms. If they want the model as counselor, a junior in the chair, or a brain hosted on our side, we refuse.

**What stays out of the arm.** GROUNDED. Adventure Within. Seer (personal OS). Rutter. Monitor. Novel catalog.
